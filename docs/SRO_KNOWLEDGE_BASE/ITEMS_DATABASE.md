## 💰 Price Trends

### Economy Factors

**Inflation:**
- Gold loses value over time
- Prices increase naturally

**Server Age:**
- **New server:** Low prices, abundant items
- **Old server:** High prices, scarcity

**Supply vs Demand:**
- **High demand items (SOSun):** Very expensive
- **Low demand (low degree):** Cheap
