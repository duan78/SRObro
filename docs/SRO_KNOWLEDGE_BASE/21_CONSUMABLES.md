## 🎯 Vue d'Ensemble

Les **consommables** sont des items à usage unique qui fournissent des effets temporaires ou des restaurations. Ils sont essentiels pour la survie et l'efficacité dans Silkroad Online.

### Points Clés
- ✅ **Potions:** HP/MP restoration
- ✅ **Scrolls:** Teleport, speed, buffs
- ✅ **Pills:** Vigor, special effects
- ✅ **Buffs:** Attack, defense increases

---

## 🧪 Potions

### HP Potions

**Types:**

| Type | HP Restore | Level Req | Prix |
|------|------------|-----------|------|
| **Small HP Potion** | ~100-200 | 1 | ~50 gold |
| **Medium HP Potion** | ~300-500 | 20 | ~200 gold |
| **Large HP Potion** | ~600-1000 | 40 | ~500 gold |
| **Universal Pill (HP)** | ~1500-2500 | 60 | ~2000 gold |

**Usage:**
- Critical en combat
- Grinding
- PvP
- Emergency healing

**Strategy:**
- Always carry potions
- Use before you're too low
- Don't wait until 10% HP

### MP Potions

**Types:**

| Type | MP Restore | Level Req | Prix |
|------|------------|-----------|------|
| **Small MP Potion** | ~50-100 | 1 | ~50 gold |
| **Medium MP Potion** | ~200-400 | 20 | ~200 gold |
| **Large MP Potion** | ~500-800 | 40 | ~500 gold |
| **Universal Pill (MP)** | ~1200-2000 | 60 | ~2000 gold |

**Usage:**
- Pour les nukers (INT)
- SP farming
- Skill spam

**Strategy:**
- INT builds need way more MP than HP
- Stock up!

### Universal Pills

**HP + MP combined:**
- Restore both HP and MP
- More expensive
- Convenient

## 📜 Scrolls

### Speed Scrolls

**Types:**

| Scroll | Speed Boost | Duration | Prix |
|--------|------------|----------|------|
| **Haste Scroll** | +10% | 10 min | ~10k gold |
| **Speed Scroll** | +20% | 30 min | ~50k gold |
| **Flash Speed Scroll** | +30% | 1 hour | ~200k gold |

**Usage:**
- Faster travel
- Kiting in PvP
- Escape

**Stacking:**
- Stack avec Lightning buffs (Bard)
- Can reach very high speeds!

### Return Scrolls

**Function:**
- Téléporte vers la ville la plus proche
- Instant cast
- One-time use

**Types:**
- **Return Scroll:** To nearest town
- **Reverse Return Scroll:** To your "home" city
- **Guild Return Scroll:** To your guild's fortress (si owned)

**Usage:**
- Quick escape
- Fast travel
- Return to town after grinding

**Strategy:**
- Always carry some
- Save for emergencies

### Buff Scrolls

**Attack Scroll:**
- +10-20% PHY attack
- Duration: 30-60 min

**Defense Scroll:**
- +10-20% PHY/MAG DEF
- Duration: 30-60 min

**Usage:**
- Stack avec character buffs
- Extra stats for tough fights

## 🌿 Herbs et Grains

### Herbs

**Function:**
- Instant HP restoration (sur certaines versions)
- HoT (Heal over Time) sur d'autres

**Types:**
- **Small Herb:** +100 HP over 10 sec
- **Medium Herb:** +300 HP over 10 sec
- **Large Herb:** +600 HP over 10 sec

**Usage:**
- Cheaper alternative à potions
- Post-combat healing

### Grains

**Function:**
- Instant MP restoration
- Ou MP over time

**Types:**
- **Small Grain:** +50 MP over 10 sec
- **Medium Grain:** +200 MP over 10 sec
- **Large Grain:** +500 MP over 10 sec

**Usage:**
- Pour INT builds
- SP farming (MP efficiency)

## 💊 Pills

### Vigor Pills

**Function:**
- Restores "Vigor" (stamina)
- Permet de sprinter plus longtemps

**Usage:**
- Long-distance travel
- Kiting
- Escape

### Universal Pills

**Function:**
- Combined HP + MP restoration
- Most efficient healing

**Stats:**
- HP: +1500-2500
- MP: +800-1500
- Instant use

**Usage:**
- Emergency healing
- PvP
- Boss fights

## 🔮 Buffs

### Attack Buffs

**Types:**
1. **Attack Increase Scroll:** +10-20% PHY attack
2. **Wizard's Attack Buff:** +MAG attack
3. **Bard Tuning:** +Attack (AOE)

**Duration:** 30-60 minutes

**Usage:**
- Before tough fights
- Grinding
- PvP

### Defense Buffs

**Types:**
1. **Defense Increase Scroll:** +10-20% DEF
2. **Cleric Bless:** +PHY DEF
3. **Bard buffs:** Various defensive buffs

**Duration:** 30-60 minutes

**Usage:**
- Tanking
- Survivabilité

### Mana Buffs

**Types:**
1. **Mana Increase Scroll:** +20% MP pool
2. **Bard Noise:** +MP

**Duration:** 30-60 minutes

**Usage:**
- INT builds
- SP farming
- Long grinding sessions

## 🎯 Usage Stratégique

### Pour Grinding

**Carry:**
- 100-500 HP potions (selon la durée)
- 200-1000 MP potions (pour INT)
- 10-20 Return Scrolls
- Speed scrolls (optionnel)

**Usage:**
- Use HP potions at 70-80% HP
- Use MP potions proactively (don't run out)
- Return when full/broken gear

### Pour PvP

**Carry:**
- 50-100 HP potions (high quality!)
- 20-50 MP potions
- Universal Pills (emergency)
- Buff scrolls

**Usage:**
- Potions are ESSENTIAL in PvP
- Don't be ashamed to pot
- Use pills when low

### Pour SP Farming

**Carry:**
- **TONS of MP potions** (INT builds)
- Moderate HP potions
- MP buff scrolls
- Garment armor (-20% MP usage)

**Strategy:**
- MP efficiency is everything
- Use skills with low MP cost
- Buffs help reduce MP consumption

## ❓ FAQ

### Q: Les potions ont-elles un cooldown?
**R:** Sur iSRO classique, non. Vous pouvez spam des potions. Sur certains serveurs privés, oui.

### Q: Les buff scrolls stack-elles avec les buffs de classe?
**R:** Généralement oui. Vous pouvez avoir attack buff (classe) + attack scroll.

### Q: Les consumables sont-ils chers?
**R:** Ça dépend. Les potions de base sont pas chères. Les high-level consumables peuvent être très chers.

### Q: Puis-ai-ai vendre des consumables?
**R:** Oui, beaucoup de joueurs achètent des consumables plutôt que de farmer les mobs.
